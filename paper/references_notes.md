# Reference verification notes

All references in paper.md were checked in October 2026 against Crossref/OpenAlex, Scopus (via NSK ezproxy) or the journal page.

* Scopus searches run (advanced search, 16 Oct 2026): (1) `TITLE-ABS-KEY(Croatia AND tourism AND (seasonality OR "seasonal concentration" OR "Gini coefficient"))` - 45 documents; (2) `TITLE-ABS-KEY(tourism AND (Knin OR "Sibenik-Knin" OR "Dalmatian hinterland" OR "Zagora" OR "Krka National Park" OR "continental Croatia" OR "inland Croatia"))` - 31 documents; (3) `TITLE-ABS-KEY(("eVisitor" OR "e-Visitor") AND (tourism OR tourist)) OR TITLE-ABS-KEY(Croatia AND tourism AND (Schengen OR "euro adoption" OR "euro area" OR "eurozone"))` - 19 documents; (4) `TITLE-ABS-KEY(Croatia AND (tourist OR tourism) AND (forecast* OR ARIMA OR SARIMA OR "exponential smoothing" OR "Holt-Winters" OR "structural break" OR "time series")) AND PUBYEAR > 2012` - 39 documents.

No study quantifying the effect of Croatia's 2023 euro/Schengen accession on tourist arrivals was found; the paper says so explicitly.
